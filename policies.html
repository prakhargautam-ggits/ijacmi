Policies

Charges, copyright and licensing

Everything about money and rights, stated in one place.

Article processing charges

Charges

Volume 1 carries no charges of any kind, for any author.

From Volume 2, a processing charge will apply. The amount will be published on this page before the call for that volume opens, and will not change for a manuscript once it has been submitted.

There is no submission fee. Nothing is ever charged before a paper has been accepted, and a charge is never a factor in an editorial decision. Waivers are available on request for authors without institutional or grant funding, and a waiver request has no bearing on how a manuscript is assessed.

Copyright

Who owns the work

Authors retain copyright of their work in full. Authors grant IJACMI a non-exclusive right to publish and distribute the article.

We do not ask for a copyright transfer, we do not require an exclusive licence, and we reserve no commercial rights. You may republish your own work anywhere, at any time, without asking us.

Licensing

How readers may use it

All articles are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Anyone may read, download, distribute and build upon the work, including commercially, provided the original is credited.

The licence statement is printed on every article and embedded in the metadata of every deposit.

Preservation

Archiving

The version of record is deposited in Zenodo, a repository operated by CERN, where it remains available independently of this journal and of its publisher.

Authors may deposit their article in any institutional or subject repository at any time, without embargo, in any version.

Corrections. Published articles are not silently altered. An error that affects the findings is addressed by a corrigendum published as a separate item with its own DOI, linked from the original.
