Volume 1, Issue 1

Call for Papers

The founding issue of IJACMI is open for submissions. No charges apply to any author in this volume.

The call

What we are looking for

IJACMI invites original, unpublished research in advanced computing and machine intelligence for its founding issue. We are a new journal, and we are building the first volume deliberately rather than filling it.

Alongside conventional research papers we specifically invite replication studies and negative results — careful work showing that a published method does not hold up, or that a reasonable approach failed. These are hard to place elsewhere and we would rather they existed in the record than not.

  • Machine learning
  • Deep learning
  • Natural language processing
  • Computer vision
  • Speech and multimodal systems
  • Low-resource and Indic languages
  • Graph representation learning
  • Explainability and evaluation
  • Intelligent and autonomous systems
  • Applied AI — health, agriculture, education
  • Efficient and edge computing

Terms

What publishing with us means

No charges

Volume 1 carries no article processing charge and no submission fee, for any author, with no conditions attached.

A permanent DOI

Every accepted article receives a DOI registered through Zenodo, operated by CERN. It is reserved before typesetting and printed on the article itself.

You keep copyright

Authors retain copyright in full. Publication is under CC BY 4.0. There is no copyright transfer form.

Real review

Double-blind, at least two independent reviewers, and the full reviewer comments passed to you unedited.

Before you submit

Where this journal stands

We would rather you decided on accurate information than on a confident pitch, so plainly:

  • We are new. This is our first issue.
  • We hold no indexing in Scopus, Web of Science or UGC-CARE, and we claim none. We intend to apply to DOAJ after twelve months of continuous publishing.
  • Our ISSN application follows publication of the first issue, as Indian regulations require for online publications.
  • Your article will carry a permanent DOI and will remain available in Zenodo independently of this journal.

If you need a Scopus-indexed venue for a thesis or promotion requirement, this is not yet that journal. We would rather say so now than have you find out after publication.

How to submit

Sending your paper

Send your manuscript as a single PDF to [submissions email] with the subject line IJACMI Vol 1 — Submission. You will receive an acknowledgement with a submission reference within two working days.

Full manuscript requirements are on the For Authors page. In brief: 6 to 15 pages, anonymised, abstract of 150–250 words, similarity below 15 percent excluding references.
